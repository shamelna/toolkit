## 📊 7 QC Tools - Quality Control Toolkit

### **🔗 Access:** [https://toolkit.kaizenacademy.education/7-QC-Tools/](https://toolkit.kaizenacademy.education/7-QC-Tools/)

### **📋 Description**
Complete quality control toolkit implementing the 7 fundamental Quality Control tools as defined by Kaoru Ishikawa. Built for statistical process control and quality improvement initiatives.

### **🛠️ Core Features**

#### **Histogram & Cpk Analysis**
- **Distribution Analysis**: Visualize data distribution patterns
- **Process Capability**: Calculate Cp, Cpk indices
- **Specification Limits**: Upper and lower spec limit analysis
- **Statistical Summary**: Mean, standard deviation, min/max values
- **Professional Reports**: PDF export with Kaizen Academy branding

#### **Pareto Chart Analysis**
- **80/20 Rule**: Identify vital few vs. trivial many
- **Priority Analysis**: Focus on most significant factors
- **Cumulative Analysis**: Running total percentages
- **Category Management**: Customizable categories and labels
- **Visual Insights**: Color-coded impact levels

#### **Control Charts**
- **SPC Monitoring**: Statistical Process Control charts
- **X-bar & R Charts**: Variable data control charts
- **P & C Charts**: Attribute data control charts
- **Process Stability**: Detect process shifts and trends
- **Control Limits**: Calculate and display UCL/LCL

#### **Fishbone Diagram**
- **Root Cause Analysis**: Ishikawa diagram for problem solving
- **6M Categories**: Man, Machine, Method, Material, Measurement, Mother Nature
- **Interactive Builder**: Drag-and-drop interface
- **Export Options**: Save as image or PDF
- **Team Collaboration**: Share diagrams for group analysis

#### **Scatter Diagrams**
- **Correlation Analysis**: Identify relationships between variables
- **Regression Lines**: Linear trend analysis
- **Correlation Coefficient**: Calculate r-squared values
- **Outlier Detection**: Identify unusual data points
- **Predictive Insights**: Forecast based on correlations

#### **Check Sheets**
- **Data Collection**: Structured data gathering templates
- **Defect Tracking: Monitor quality issues
- **Frequency Analysis**: Count occurrences by category
- **Time-based Tracking**: Monitor trends over time
- **Customizable Forms**: Tailored to specific processes

#### **PDF Reporting**
- **Professional Reports**: Kaizen Academy branded PDFs
- **Executive Summaries**: Plain-language interpretations
- **Statistical Analysis**: Detailed numerical results
- **Visual Charts**: Embedded graphs and diagrams
- **Course Integration**: Links to relevant training courses

## 🧪 DOE Navigator - Design of Experiments

### **🔗 Access:** [https://toolkit.kaizenacademy.education/DOE-Navigator/](https://toolkit.kaizenacademy.education/DOE-Navigator/)

### **📋 Description**
Complete Design of Experiments wizard implementing Montgomery's 7-stage DOE methodology. Provides guided experiment design, statistical analysis, and optimization tools.

### **🛠️ Core Features**

#### **7-Stage Wizard Methodology**
1. **Problem Definition**: Clear problem formulation
2. **Response Variables**: Select and define outputs
3. **Factors & Levels**: Identify experimental variables
4. **Design Recommendation**: Get optimal design suggestions
5. **Execution Planning**: Run experiment protocols
6. **Analysis Engine**: Statistical analysis tools
7. **Conclusions**: Interpret and document results

#### **Design Recommendation Engine**
- **Montgomery Framework**: Industry-standard DOE approach
- **Factor Selection**: Choose appropriate experimental factors
- **Level Optimization**: Determine optimal factor levels
- **Design Types**: Full factorial, fractional, Taguchi, response surface
- **Sample Size**: Calculate required experimental runs

#### **Statistical Analysis**
- **ANOVA**: Analysis of variance for factor significance
- **Effects Analysis**: Main effects and interactions
- **Regression Models**: Build predictive equations
- **Residual Analysis**: Check model assumptions
- **Optimization**: Find optimal factor settings

#### **Response Surface Methodology**
- **3D Visualization**: Surface plots of responses
- **Contour Maps**: 2D representation of optimal regions
- **Desirability Functions**: Multi-response optimization
- **Prediction Intervals**: Confidence in predictions
- **Robustness Analysis**: Sensitivity to variations

#### **Professional Reporting**
- **Executive Summary**: Plain-language results interpretation
- **Statistical Details**: Complete analysis documentation
- **Visual Charts**: Graphical representation of results
- **Recommendations**: Actionable next steps
- **Methodology Documentation**: Experimental design details

### **🎯 Target Users**
- Process Engineers
- Research & Development Teams
- Quality Improvement Specialists
- Manufacturing Engineers
- Six Sigma Black Belts
